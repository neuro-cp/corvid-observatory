# Research milestones

1. **Establish provenance.** One public-domain crow clip and a CC-BY 4.0 captive-rook annotation archive are included with individual source credits and hashes. They cannot be treated as matched audio and events. Expand the corpus with recordings that include documented contexts.
2. **Inspect sounds.** Produce reproducible waveform and spectrogram plots; manually mark candidate call intervals and assess recording quality. Acoustic features are observations, not semantic labels.
3. **Collect independent context.** Design time-synchronized observations with separate event and call annotation, including no-call sessions and recording effort. No predator identity or behavioral context is known for the initial sample.
4. **Measure reliability.** Compare independent annotators on a subset and track uncertain labels; review misalignments before analysis.
5. **Pre-register and validate.** Specify a testable prediction, hold out whole sessions and sites, and compare with simple baselines. Account for repeated calls within sessions and report uncertainty; avoid claiming causal meaning from associations.
6. **Report reproducibly.** Publish methods, provenance, negative results, limitations, and safe data-sharing practices. Avoid disturbing wildlife or disclosing sensitive nest locations.

The rook archive supplies context annotations but does not independently establish causal interpretation; its full audio is not yet included. Milestones 3–6 require additional review of sampling and annotations and appropriate held-out observations.
