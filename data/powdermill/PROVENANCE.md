The source README says recordings were sampled at 32 kHz, but this selected MP3 and WAV both decode at **44,100 Hz**, for 300 seconds. The plotter uses the decoded sample rate. A comparison of five five-second windows found zero-sample peak offset between WAV and MP3 within a ±0.5 s search; see [alignment results](../../results/POWDERMILL_ALIGNMENT.md) for scope and limitations. The authors report that three researchers contributed to the annotations and that identifiable vocalizations except short chips were annotated. These labels are not exhaustive detections of all sounds, and no behavior, individual identity, predator event, or meaning is provided.
